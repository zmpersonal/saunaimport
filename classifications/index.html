Sauna tariff classifications

There is no single universal U.S. HTS number for every sauna. Classification depends on what is imported, how complete it is, whether it is freestanding, what provides its essential character and sometimes how components are entered together.

Short answer

CBP precedent shows at least four materially different paths: a complete freestanding wooden sauna can fall in heading 9406; a non-freestanding wood kit may have its components classified separately; an electric sauna heater has been classified in 8516.29; and a portable infrared sauna has been classified in 8516.79.

N304393 · 2019

Complete wooden sauna building

9406.10.0000

A weather-resistant freestanding wooden sauna was classified as a prefabricated building of wood.

Electric sauna heater

8516.29.00

N290059 · 2017

Built-in sauna kit: not necessarily heading 9406

CBP found that a non-freestanding kit lacking its own framing, exterior walls, roof and other building elements did not have the essential character of a prefabricated building. Wood components were classified separately.
