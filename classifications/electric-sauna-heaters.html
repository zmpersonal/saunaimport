CBP Headquarters ruling H179957

Electric sauna heaters & HTS 8516.29.00

CBP concluded that an electric sauna heater was properly classified as other electric space-heating apparatus. The other items in the DIY cedar sauna kit were to be classified separately.

Classification in the ruling

8516.29.00

The ruling concerned the electric sauna heater within a multi-component DIY cedar sauna kit.

Why this matters for sauna trade research

Heaters can move through a different tariff category from sauna cabins or structures. A model of the sauna industry that tracks only prefabricated wooden buildings will miss heater trade; a model that simply adds the entire electric-space-heating category will overstate sauna trade because the heading includes non-sauna equipment.

Data limitation

Sauna Import tracks the corresponding current Census category as a broad indicator and keeps it separate from other proxy categories.

Do not infer heater unit counts from category dollars. The statistical category is not sauna-specific and may include products with very different values.

U.S. Customs and Border Protection CROSS ruling H179957.
