CBP Headquarters ruling H179957

Electric sauna heaters & HTS 8516.29

CBP concluded that the electric sauna heater in a DIY cedar sauna kit was other electric space-heating apparatus. The other kit items were not automatically absorbed into the heater classification.

Short answer

H179957 placed the electric sauna heater in subheading 8516.29.00. Sauna Import tracks Census statistical reporting number 8516.29.0090 for the current broad proxy series; the extra digits are a statistical breakout, not evidence that every product in the series is a sauna heater.

Classification in H179957

8516.29.00

Current Census tracking on this site: 8516.29.0090.

Why the kit did not become one tariff article

The ruling concerned a non-freestanding DIY cedar sauna kit containing wood pieces, hardware, barriers, a door and an electric heater. CBP treated the heater and other components separately rather than classifying the entire collection as one prefabricated building.

8 digits vs. 10 digits

The HTSUS can state a legal subheading at the 8-digit level while Census import statistics use a 10-digit statistical reporting number below it. That is why the ruling citation and the data endpoint can display slightly different-looking codes without representing a contradiction.

What the trade data means

The 8516.29.0090 series includes other electric space-heating apparatus beyond sauna heaters. It is useful for category direction and origin mix, not for estimating sauna-heater units or revenue.

Duty treatment is separate from classification. The base HTS rate, country of origin and any applicable Chapter 99 or trade-remedy measures should be checked for the actual entry date.

Primary source

U.S. Customs and Border Protection CROSS ruling H179957.
